Sage

Menopause Symptom Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026  ·  Effective: May 25, 2026

Short version: Sage stores all your data on your iPhone only. We collect no personal information, run no analytics, and send nothing anywhere. Your health data never leaves your device.

1. Who We Are

Sage is an iOS application developed by Anulfo Acosta, an independent developer ("we", "us", "our"). Our contact email is anulfito1991@gmail.com.

2. What Data Sage Collects

Sage collects only the information you voluntarily enter into the app:

  • Symptom entries (hot flash count, mood, energy, sleep quality, brain fog, etc.)
  • HRT regimen details (type, dose, start date, compliance)
  • Menopause life stage and tracked symptom preferences
  • Trigger and management action entries
  • Journal notes

If you grant Apple Health access, Sage also reads sleep and heart rate data from HealthKit. This data is displayed in the app and is never transmitted to any server.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. Data is protected by iOS's built-in encryption (Data Protection) and is tied to your device passcode / Face ID / Touch ID.

We do not operate any server, cloud database, or backend. We have no access to your data.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account credentials
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No crash reports or analytics events
  • No location data
  • No third-party tracking or behavioral profiling

5. Apple HealthKit

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Sage requests read-only access to sleep analysis, resting heart rate, body mass, and basal body temperature. Sage does not write to Apple Health. HealthKit data is used only within the app to enrich your symptom insights and is never shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transmitted off your device. This is a requirement of Apple's HealthKit guidelines and is enforced technically by the app's design.

6. Subscriptions

Sage Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store via StoreKit. We receive only what Apple shares with developers (no payment card details, no full name). Apple's Privacy Policy governs that transaction.

7. Push Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Sage schedules these as local notifications on your device — they are generated by the app itself and do not involve any server. The app uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) infrastructure only if you enable trial-end reminders, in which case a push token is registered with APNs under Apple's standard infrastructure. No message content is stored on any server we operate.

8. PDF Reports

The 90-day PDF report is generated entirely on your device and is shared only when you explicitly tap "Export" and choose a destination (AirDrop, email, files, etc.). We have no access to the report content.

9. Children's Privacy

Sage is designed for adults experiencing menopause or perimenopause. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has used the app and entered health information, you can delete all data by uninstalling the app.

10. Deleting Your Data

To delete all data stored by Sage, uninstall the app from your iPhone. All SwiftData records are deleted automatically by iOS upon uninstall. No data is retained by us because we never had access to it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if our privacy practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at anulfito1991@gmail.com.
