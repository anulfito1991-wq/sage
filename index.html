Sage

Your private menopause companion. Track symptoms, spot patterns, and walk into every doctor's appointment prepared.

All your data. Only on your iPhone.

Sage stores everything locally using Apple's SwiftData. No account required. No cloud sync. No analytics. Your health data never leaves your device.

Symptom Calendar

Doctor Reports

Export a 90-day PDF your specialist can read in 2 minutes. Premium feature.

Track how your symptom load correlates with your HRT regimen over time.
