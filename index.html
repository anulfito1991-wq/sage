Frequently asked questions

Is Sage free?

Yes. Core tracking is free forever — hot flashes, mood, sleep, night sweats, and basic 7-day charts. Sage Premium ($6.99/month or $49.99/year) unlocks 90-day doctor-ready PDF reports, 30-day advanced trend charts, and the HRT correlation view. A 7-day free trial is included with every new Premium subscription.

How is Sage different from other menopause apps?

Sage is the only major menopause tracker with 100% on-device storage — no account, no cloud, no analytics. It also offers HRT correlation tracking, a daily symptom burden score, trigger and management logging, and a doctor-formatted PDF export that competitors don't provide in this combination.

Does Sage connect to Apple Health?

Optionally. Sage can read your sleep hours and resting heart rate from Apple Health to pre-fill your daily log and enrich your trends. It never writes to Apple Health, and HealthKit data stays on your device.

Can I track perimenopause symptoms?

Yes — Sage is built for all stages: perimenopause, menopause, and post-menopause. During perimenopause it also tracks cycle status (period, spotting, or none) alongside all other symptoms.

Can I use Sage to track my HRT?

Yes. You log your HRT type, dose, start date, and frequency during setup. Each daily log includes an HRT compliance checkbox. Premium users see a before/after chart showing how hot flash frequency correlates with their HRT regimen.

Is my health data private?

Completely. Sage has no server and no account. All your symptoms, journal notes, HRT details, and Apple Health data are stored only on your iPhone in encrypted local storage. We have zero access to your data.

Is Sage a medical app?

No. Sage is a personal wellness journal — it does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your symptoms and treatment options. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
